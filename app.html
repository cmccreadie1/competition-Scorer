WHAT IS SHORESCORE?

ShoreScore is a zero-latency digital scorecard for beach fishing. It works instantly across all devices.

Competitors: Join a match, log your fish, and watch the live leaderboard update in real-time. If you lose phone signal on the beach, keep logging! The app safely saves your fish and will automatically sync them the second your signal returns.

Stewards: Host a match, set the rules, and manage the tournament from your own device.

HOW TO START OR JOIN

Every tournament revolves around a 4-Digit Room PIN. The Steward creates the match and receives the PIN. They shout this PIN down the beach or post it in your WhatsApp group.

Competitors tap "Join as Competitor" and enter that exact PIN to link their phones to the tournament.

LEAVING EARLY

If you need to pack up before the clock hits zero, just open the Options menu and tap Leave Match Early. It is completely safe. All of your logged fish are permanently saved to the Steward's master scorecard, and you can check the final tournament results later from the View Past Matches button on the home screen.

👑 FULL STEWARD'S MANUAL

1. The Command Bridge:
During a match, tap the [ ⚙️ OPTIONS & EXIT ] button at the top of your screen. From here, you can adjust the match clock, trigger a 3-minute "lines up" grace period, or hand over Steward control to someone else if your battery is dying.

2. God Mode Audits:
Need to fix an angler's mistake? From the Options menu, tap Review Angler Logs. Select any angler to view their diary. You have the power to Log a Catch directly onto their scorecard, or permanently Delete a bogus fish from the global leaderboard.

3. The Official Report:
When the clock hits zero, tap Share Official Report. The app generates a plain-text summary of the final podium and match stats that you can send straight to WhatsApp.

Survival Guide

Don't panic! Here is everything you need to know to log your fish safely.

How do I log a fish?

  1. Tap the "Select Fish Species" box and pick your catch.
  2. Tap the Measurement box. A giant number pad will appear. Type in the size.
  3. Hit the bold blue ADD button. Your phone will vibrate to confirm the fish is saved and synced.

I made a mistake. Can I fix it?

Yes. Look at your Match Journal below. Next to every fish you log, there is an Edit / Delete button.

  • Tap Edit / Delete to open the manage menu.
  • Update the fish size or species and tap SAVE CHANGES.
  • Tap Delete Fish to remove it entirely.

How do I check the scores?

The blue "Island" at the very top of your screen shows your current Rank and Points. To see everyone else, tap the floating CLICK FOR LIVE LEADERBOARD button at the bottom of your screen. Hint: You can tap anyone's name on that leaderboard to peek at exactly what they are catching!

Will this drain my battery?

No. ShoreScore does not have a "sleep" button. When you are done logging a fish, just press your phone's physical power button to turn the screen off and drop it in your pocket. The exact second you wake your screen up, the app will instantly reconnect and download any new fish caught by the rest of the beach.

What if I need to leave early?

If you need to pack up before the clock hits zero, open the Options menu and tap Leave Match Early. It is completely safe. All of your logged fish are permanently saved to the Steward's master scorecard, and you can check the final tournament results later from your History Vault.
