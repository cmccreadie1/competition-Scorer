SHORESCORE

ShoreScore is a live digital scorecard built for casual shore anglers. Whether you are keeping tally in a relaxed match between friends or just out scratching on a solo session, the app logs your catches instantly and works flawlessly offline.

Lose signal on the beach? Keep logging. The app safely stores your fish and syncs the second your connection returns.

Explore Features

Choose Your Mode

Create Match: Host a live leaderboard and set the rules for multiple anglers.

Join Match: Enter a 4-digit Room PIN provided by your Steward to link your phone to the live board.

Session Diary (Solo): Fish alone. Track your personal catches, environmental data, and venue stats without needing a lobby.

Venue Intelligence & Tides

The app features a powerful mathematical engine that correlates every fish you catch with the exact state of the tide. Just do a quick web search for your chosen mark's High and Low water times, type them in, and the app handles the rest—unlocking Tidal Wave Heatmaps, Flow Rate Bias, and Species Peak Windows.

3-Second Catch Logging

No typing, no squinting, and no confusing menus. Built specifically for freezing, wet hands. Just pick your fish from a simple list, tap the size on a massive number pad, and hit the big blue 'ADD' button. It is as easy as making a phone call.

The Official Report

When you finish a match or a solo session, the app generates a clinical, plain-text summary formatted perfectly for sharing to WhatsApp or saving in your History Vault. It delivers raw data: Final Standings, Golden Hour, First Blood, and deep tidal analytics, allowing you to build a permanent database of exactly how and when a venue fishes best.

Impossible to Break

You cannot "mess up" the match. If you accidentally log the wrong fish or make a typo, you can easily fix or delete it right from your diary with one tap.

Pack Up Early Safely

Need to beat the traffic or get out of the rain? Tap 'Leave Early' to safely lock your catches in. Your score is permanently saved, and you can check the final results from home.

The 'Glance' Display

Keep your phone in your pocket. When you wake your screen, a clean display at the very top instantly shows your current rank, points, and the time remaining.

Never Get Lost

If you ever feel stuck or forget how to do something, help is always just one tap away. The app includes a simple App Guide on the home screen, plus a handy "Survival Guide" during the match to answer any questions instantly.
